# Retail Customer Behavior Analysis: End-to-End Data Analytics Project (Python, SQL, Power BI)
## Business Problem

A retail company is experiencing shifts in customer purchasing behavior across:
- demographics
- product categories
- online vs offline channels

The key challenge:
>> How can the company use its data to understand customer behavior, improve sales, and increase long-term customer loyalty?

## Project Objective

This project transforms raw retail data into actionable insights by:
- Identifying key drivers of customer purchasing decisions
- Segmenting customers based on behavior
- Evaluating the impact of discounts, subscriptions, and shipping
- Delivering data-driven recommendations for business growth

## Tools & Technologies
- Python (Pandas, NumPy) → Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering
- PostgreSQL → Data Analysis & Business Queries
- Power BI → Data Visualization & Dashboarding

## Dataset Overview
- Records: 3,900 transactions
- Features: 18 columns
  Includes:
    - Customer demographics (Age, Gender, Location, Subscription Status)
    - Purchase details (Product, Category, Amount, Season)
    - Behavioral data (Discounts, Reviews, Frequency, Shipping Type)

## Project Workflow
### 1. Data Preparation & Modeling (Python)

- Cleaned and transformed raw dataset
- Handled missing values (Review Ratings imputed using category median)
- Standardized column naming (snake_case)
- Performed feature engineering:
  - Customer age groups
  - Purchase frequency
- Removed redundant fields
- Loaded clean dataset into PostgreSQL

### 2. Data Analysis (SQL)

Answered key business questions such as:
- Who drives the most revenue?
- Do discounts increase spending or reduce margins?
- Are subscribed customers more valuable?
- Which products perform best by rating and sales?
- How does shipping type affect purchase behavior?
Also performed:
- Customer segmentation (New, Returning, Loyal)
- Behavioral pattern analysis
- Revenue breakdown across demographics

### 3. Data Visualization (Power BI)

Built an interactive dashboard to:
- Track revenue by customer segments
- Analyze purchasing patterns
- Compare product and category performance
- Evaluate impact of discounts, subscriptions, and shipping

## Key Insights
- Customer segments behave differently — a single strategy is ineffective
- Discounts increase purchases but may reduce profitability
- Loyal and subscribed customers contribute significantly to revenue
- Product ratings strongly influence purchasing decisions
- Shipping type impacts customer spending behavior

## Business Recommendations
- Boost Subscriptions → Increase customer lifetime value
- Strengthen Loyalty Programs → Convert repeat buyers into loyal customers
- Optimize Discount Strategy → Balance revenue growth with profitability
- Improve Product Positioning → Promote top-rated & best-selling products
- Target Marketing Campaigns → Focus on high-value customer segments
